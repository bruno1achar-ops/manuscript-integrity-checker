Interpretation and limitations

  • This tool searches scholarly metadata and available abstracts; it does not compare against every journal article, thesis, book, website, subscription database or student paper.
  • A match may reflect standard terminology, a properly cited quotation, methods language or common academic phrasing.
  • Paraphrased text may not be detected. A missing match does not establish originality.
  • The flagged-word estimate is an internal screening indicator. It is not equivalent to a Turnitin or iThenticate similarity score.
  • Always inspect the original source and citation context before making editorial or academic-integrity decisions.
