The Manuscript Studio

Manuscript Integrity Suite

Scholarly similarity screening, passage review, reference auditing, and quality checks.

Scope and limitations: This tool searches scholarly metadata and compares selected manuscript passages with retrieved titles and abstracts. It is not Turnitin or iThenticate, does not cover all published full text, and cannot determine whether plagiarism occurred. A zero-match result is not proof of originality. Human review is essential.
